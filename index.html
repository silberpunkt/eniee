Eniee monad

(define (sqrt x)
  (define (good-enough? guess)
    (< (abs (- (square guess) x)) 0.001))
  (define (improve guess)
    (average guess (/ x guess)))
  (define (sqrt-iter guess)
    (if (good-enough? guess)
        guess
        (sqrt-iter (improve guess))))
  (sqrt-iter 1.0))

Two dashes

There is no en dash. Type the en dash itself, and the font says something: – The em dash is the hyphen made twice over: a—b. Hyphens typed in a row stay hyphens, as typed: -- and ---. Nothing is changed behind your back.
